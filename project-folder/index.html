About the archive

Alexandria Archive & Learning Hub is a public learning institution serving readers, students, and researchers. Our collections bring together regional history, scholarly works, and contemporary voices in a welcoming space for inquiry and community exchange.

We preserve original records, make trustworthy research resources easier to discover, and host free programs that invite lifelong learning.

A library is not a luxury but one of the necessities of life.

— Henry Ward Beecher

Facilities & terminology

Reading rooms and facilities

  1. Main Reading Hall: A quiet study space with reference desks and accessible seating.
  2. Map Room: A supervised space for historic maps, atlases, and geographic research.
  3. History and Travel Room: Open shelves for regional history, biographies, and travel writing.
  4. Special Collections Room: Staff-assisted access to rare and fragile materials.
  5. Community Learning Studio: A flexible room for workshops, talks, and small-group study.

Library terminology

Call number
A shelf address that groups an item by subject and helps readers find it in the collection.
Hold status
The current state of a requested item, such as available for pickup, waiting, or checked out.
Non-circulating reference
A reference work that must be consulted in the library and cannot be borrowed for home use.
