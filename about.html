My story

Build with care. Communicate with purpose.

As an ITI graduate, I developed a practical foundation in software development and a lasting interest in how well-designed systems solve everyday problems. I pay particular attention to object-oriented programming, memory management, and modular code: the foundations that help a project stay understandable as it grows.

My academic background in English Literature and Language, with a minor in translation and content creation, complements that technical perspective. I enjoy making technical ideas clear for people across languages, while keeping meaning, tone, and context in view.

Alongside my studies, community and customer-facing roles strengthened my listening, organization, and problem-solving skills. They continue to shape how I collaborate: with curiosity, care, and respect for the person on the other side of the work.
