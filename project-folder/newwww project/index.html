Our purpose

A library for every chapter.

Alexandria Archive & Learning Hub brings books, research resources, and people together. Our doors are open to curious minds of every age, background, and ambition.

From treasured stories and local history to practical digital skills, our collections invite you to follow a question wherever it leads. Librarians are here to help you navigate the shelves, the archives, and the wider world of information.

Borrow with confidence

Membership & loan periods

All memberships are free. Bring your library card when borrowing in person, or sign in online to renew eligible items.

Loan periods and renewal rules by membership tier
Membership tier Loan duration Items at a time Renewal policy
Community 21 days 10 items Up to 2 renewals of 21 days each, if no holds are waiting.
Student 28 days 15 items Up to 2 renewals of 28 days each, if no holds are waiting.
Research 42 days 25 items 1 renewal of 42 days; special collections are reading-room use only.
Renew online, by phone, or in person. Overdue items cannot be renewed while another patron has placed a hold.

Find your place

Reading halls

Choose a space that suits the way you like to learn:

  1. Grand Reading Hall — a quiet, light-filled space for individual study.
  2. Workshop Commons — flexible tables for group projects and hands-on learning.
  3. Children’s Story Room — a welcoming space for story time, play, and young readers.
  4. Archive Reading Room — supervised access to local history and special collections.

A few useful words

Library reference

Call number
A shelf address that helps you locate a specific item in the collection.
Renewal
An extension of a loan period, subject to the item’s eligibility and any waiting holds.
Interlibrary loan
A service that requests books and other materials from a participating library on your behalf.
